## Interactive features

### Progress tracker
Mark any course as "Read" by clicking the checkmark button at the top of the course page. Your progress is saved locally in your browser — no login needed. The homepage shows your overall completion percentage.

### Bookmarks
Star courses you want to come back to. Bookmarked courses appear in a dedicated section on the homepage for quick access.

### Continue where you left off
The site remembers which courses you've recently visited. The "Continue where you left off" section on the homepage shows your last-read courses so you can pick up exactly where you stopped.

### Highlight and annotate
See the sidebar on the right edge of the page? That's [Hypothes.is](https://hypothes.is) — a collaborative annotation tool.

**How to use it:**
1. Select any text in a course
2. Click "Annotate" or "Highlight" in the popup
3. Add your note — it's visible to everyone reading the same page
4. Use it to flag important concepts, add context, ask questions, or share tips

This turns every course into a living document where the community adds value over time.

### Discussion / comments
Scroll to the bottom of any course to find the **Discussion** section. It's powered by [Giscus](https://giscus.app) — comments are stored as GitHub Discussions in this repository.

**How to use it:**
1. Scroll to the "Discussion" section at the bottom of any course
2. Sign in with your GitHub account
3. Ask questions, share production war stories, suggest improvements, or help others
4. React to comments with emoji

Each course has its own discussion thread — conversations stay focused and searchable.

### Search
Press `/` or click the search icon to open the command palette. Type any keyword to instantly search across all 153 courses, categories, and tags. Results update as you type.

### Dark / light theme
Click the theme toggle in the top-right corner. Your preference is saved across sessions.

### Offline support
The site works offline after your first visit. A service worker caches the app shell and previously visited courses. Read on a plane, train, or anywhere without internet.
